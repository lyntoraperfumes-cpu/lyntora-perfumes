Lyntora Perfumes - Logo Crop Update

Admin: /store-admin/settings/
1. Choose an image.
2. Drag the image inside the fixed 3:1 rectangle.
3. Adjust Zoom.
4. Click USE CROPPED LOGO.
5. Click SAVE CHANGES.

The cropped logo is stored as a 900x300 PNG and displayed in a fixed rectangular
150x50 header slot. No database migration is required for this update.
